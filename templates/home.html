What is Feedora

A smarter way to gather feedback

Feedora is a gamified web-based survey platform designed for researchers and product teams who want to understand how users adopt and enjoy their Hedonic Motivation Systems, which are systems like such as video games, social media platforms, music streaming services, dating apps, virtual worlds etc

It combines the rigour of academic HMSAM methodology with modern gamification techniques to improve response quality and completion rates.

Gamification built in
Points, badges, progress bars, and a virtual mascot make filling surveys enjoyable — increasing completion rates and response quality.
Create HMSAM survey
38 validated questions pre-loaded across 7 constructs. Edit wording freely — construct mapping is fixed.
Automatic analytics
Cronbach's alpha, HMSAM construct scores, response charts, and per-respondent analysis — all generated automatically.

Gamification features

Make your form more interesting

Research shows gamification increases user participation and response quality. Feedora brings these techniques directly into your survey.

Virtual mascot
A friendly mascot greets respondents on entry, encourages them at the halfway point, asks them to stay if they try to leave, and thanks them on completion.
Progress bar
A customisable progress bar shows how far through the form respondents are. Choose style (solid, striped, gradient), colour, and label type.
Points system
Respondents earn points for every answer. Creators can customise point values per question type. Total points are displayed on the thank you page.
Badges & leaderboard
Registered respondents earn bronze, silver, and gold badges. A leaderboard shows top respondents — motivating continued engagement across surveys.
